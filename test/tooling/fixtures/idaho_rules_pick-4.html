How to Play

Wagers for Pick 4 start at $1. Pick your favorite four-digit number. If you’d like random numbers, ask for a quick pick. Choose your wager type and amount you want to wager. Wager types include: Exact Order, Any Order (4-way, 6-way, 12-way or 24-way), Exact/Any Order (4-way, 6-way, 12-way, or 24-way), Front Pair, Mid Pair or Back Pair.

For an extra $1 per dollar wagered, you can add Sum it up! If the sum of your Pick 4 numbers is the same as the sum of the four single-digit numbers drawn, you win. For example, if you play the Sum it up! feature and your Pick 4 numbers are 1-2-3-4, the sum of your numbers is 10. If the sum of the four numbers drawn also equals 10 (whether they match your numbers or not), then you win! 

Multi-draw tickets can be produced for up to 70 consecutive draws. Drawings are day and night, every day at 1:59 at 7:59 pm MT. This game is only played in Idaho!

Prize amounts listed are based on a $1 wager. All prizes must be claimed within 180 days from the draw date.

Sum it up! cannot be used with front, mid or back pair wager type. 

Pick 4 tickets cannot be canceled after they are processed. Responsibility for the numbers selected rests with the player, not the retailer or the Lottery.

  1. Wager Type Descriptions:
  2. Exact Order: Numbers drawn match your numbers in exact order.
  3. Any Order 4-way: Numbers drawn match your numbers in any order (3 numbers the same).
  4. Any Order 6-way: Numbers drawn match your numbers in any order (2 pairs of the same numbers).
  5. Any Order 12-way: Numbers drawn match your numbers in any order (2 numbers the same).
  6. Any Order 24-way: Numbers drawn match your numbers in any order (4 unique numbers).
  7. Exact/Any Order 4-way: All 4 numbers in exact/any order (3 numbers the same).
  8. Exact/Any Order 6-way: All 4 numbers in exact/any order (2 pairs of the same numbers).
  9. Exact/Any Order 12-way: All 4 numbers in exact/any order (2 numbers the same).
  10. Exact/Any Order 24-way: All 4 numbers in exact/any order (4 unique numbers).
  11. Front Pair: First 2 numbers in exact order (pick 2 numbers only).
  12. Mid Pair: Middle 2 numbers in exact order (pick 2 numbers only).
  13. Back Pair: Last 2 numbers in exact order (pick 2 numbers only).
