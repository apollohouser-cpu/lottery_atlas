How To Play

Each play cost $5. For each play, pick eight (8) NFL team logos out of 32. Fill in the circle next to the team logo. If you select fewer than 8 team logos, the remaining teams will be selected for you. If you want the computer to randomly select your teams, ask the retailer for a "Quick Pick" or mark the QP box on the playslip. If you want to play the same eight NFL teams for up to five (5) consecutive draws, mark the appropriate number of drawings in the Multi-Draw section. Drawings are held every Sunday at 8:00 p.m. MT.

NOTE: This is a lottery game, not sports betting.

Powerball Xs & Os results are randomly drawn and not based on, determined by, or dependent on the outcome of NFL games.

The overall odds of winning a prize in this game are 1:12. The Jackpot starts at $1,000,000. In cases of multiple prize winners, prize amounts will be split.

MAKE SURE TO SIGN YOUR TICKET PRIOR TO PRESENTING FOR PAYMENT.
Payment: You have 60 days after you claim the jackpot to determine if you want the cash or the annuity option. The Jackpot Prize is paid as an annuity of 30 payments over 29 years or, at the election of the winner, in a lump sum payment of the cash value of the annuity prize. If the winner chooses the annuity, the annual payments will be increased each year by a percentage set out in the Powerball game rules. 

If more than one player has a validated ticket matching the winning logos, the jackpot prize will be a parimutuel payout, determined by dividing the funds available for the Jackpot Prize by the number of Jackpot winning Plays. Lower-Tier, or set prizes are not a parimutuel payout unless liability limits are reached.
