How to Play

Wagers for Pick 3 start at $1. Pick your favorite three-digit number. If you’d like random numbers, ask for a quick pick. Choose your wager type and amount you want to wager. Wager types include: Exact Order, Any Order (4-way, 6-way), Exact/Any Order (4-way, 6-way), Front Pair, or Back Pair.

For an extra $1 per dollar wagered, you can add Sum it up! If the sum of your Pick 3 numbers is the same as the sum of the three single-digit numbers drawn, you win. For example, if you play the Sum it up! feature and your Pick 3 numbers are 1-2-3, the sum of your numbers is 6. If the sum of the three numbers drawn also equals 6 (whether they match your numbers or not), then you win! 

Multi-draw tickets can be produced for up to 70 consecutive draws. Drawings are day and night, every day at 1:59 at 7:59 pm MT. This game is only played in Idaho!

Sum it up! cannot be used with front or back pair wager type. 

Pick 3 tickets cannot be canceled after they are processed. Responsibility for the numbers selected rests with the player, not the retailer or the Lottery. 

  1. Wager Type Descriptions:
  2. Exact Order: Numbers drawn match your numbers in exact order.
  3. Any Order 3-way: Numbers drawn match your numbers in any order (3 numbers the same).
  4. Any Order 6-way: Numbers drawn match your numbers in any order (2 numbers the same).
  5. Exact/Any Order 3-way: All 3 numbers in exact/any order (3 numbers the same).
  6. Exact/Any Order 6-way: All 3 numbers in exact/any order (2 numbers the same).
  7. Front Pair: First 2 numbers in exact order (pick 2 numbers only).
  8. Back Pair: Last 2 numbers in exact order (pick 2 numbers only).
