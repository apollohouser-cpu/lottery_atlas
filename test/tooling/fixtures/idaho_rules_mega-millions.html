How to Play

Each play costs $5 and will automatically include a unique multiplier that will print directly on the ticket. This will multiply your non-jackpot prize by 2, 3, 4, 5 or 10 for no extra cost. Pick five numbers between 1 and 70. Pick one Mega Ball number between 1 and 24. If you’d like random numbers, ask for a quick pick. Multi-draw tickets can be produced for up to 10 consecutive draws. Drawings are Tuesday and Friday at 9:00 pm MT.

The starting annuitized prize for Mega Millions is $50 Million.

The overall odds of winning a prize in this game are 1:23

Mega Millions tickets cannot be canceled after they are processed. Responsibility for the numbers selected rests with the player, not the retailer or the Lottery.

MAKE SURE TO SIGN YOUR TICKET PRIOR TO PRESENTING FOR PAYMENT.
Payment: You have 60 days after you claim the jackpot to determine if you want cash or the annuity option. If you choose the annuity option you will receive your prize over 30 payments. The jackpot is pari-mutuel. You win other cash prizes when you correctly match other Mega Millions numbers drawn. Prizes must be claimed within 180 days from the date of the drawing. All prizes for Mega Millions must be claimed in the state where the ticket was purchased.
