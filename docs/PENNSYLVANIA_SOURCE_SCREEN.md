# Pennsylvania source screen — September 12, 2026

Pennsylvania is **deferred for the launch heat map** under the existing
official-source gate. Keep the existing source links, draw schedules, and
historical activity. Do not add fabricated or inferred retailer points.
The app now permits slower official publishing cadences when disclosed per
state. A one-time export must be labeled as a dated snapshot, not an
automatically updated feed.

- The [official Scratch-Off prizes remaining page](https://www.palottery.pa.gov/Scratch-Offs/Prizes-Remaining.aspx)
  publishes active game numbers, names, prices, top prize tiers, and remaining
  prize counts. This portion of the gate has a usable public source.
- The [official Where to Buy page](https://www.palottery.pa.gov/About-PA-Lottery/Retailers.aspx)
  is a location search. Its current interface offers 1- and 5-mile radii, and
  its JavaScript calls a coordinate-and-distance search endpoint. This is not
  a published, independently verifiable complete active statewide retailer
  directory or export.
- The [official Winners page](https://www.palottery.pa.gov/Winners/Winners.aspx)
  offers 2018–2026 winner lists by county, game, year, and month. Its displayed
  rows contain winner name and prize amount, not the exact selling retailer.
  The [Winners Map](https://www.palottery.pa.gov/Winners/Winners-Map.aspx)
  likewise presents county-level winner totals and rows rather than a complete
  retailer-address claims feed. Individual announcements can support specific
  historical points, but cannot establish comprehensive January 2026 onward
  retailer-level coverage.

To resume: obtain refreshable official access to the full active-retailer
directory with exact addresses and verified coordinates, plus comprehensive
winner-location records with game, prize, date, and selling retailer identifier
or exact address. Validate source-specific timestamps and disclose the actual
publishing cadence for each dataset before enabling Pennsylvania as launch-ready.
