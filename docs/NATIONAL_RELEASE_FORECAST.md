# National coverage readiness and forecast — October 6, 2026

Full public release requires usable accepted coverage in every lottery state;
limited-state iOS/Android beta can proceed earlier. NASPL currently identifies
[45 U.S. lottery states](https://www.naspl.org/faq). The repository has a source
screen for each of those 45. Eight are accepted, leaving 37. This state-based
ledger does not silently count DC or territories as states or as accepted coverage.

## Evidence and forecast limits

This is a repository-readiness audit, not 37 fresh live-source acceptances. It uses
state source screens, configured refresh jobs, generated snapshots and the
October 6 scheduled refresh status. Nineteen remaining states have recurring
Scratch catalogs; North Carolina has selected winner activity; Missouri has a
single-game total. Sixteen have no configured recurring state refresh job.
A catalog or historic point alone does not satisfy supported state acceptance.
New Hampshire is retained after failure, not a successfully refreshed catalog.
Agency records and past audits can reduce effort but are not approved public data.

Recent October 1–6 completion pace was roughly one accepted state per day, but
those states had substantial prior work. Extrapolating that pace to unaudited
states would understate risk. The working planning range is **2–3 elapsed days
per remaining state**, or **74–111 days from October 6: December 19, 2026 through
January 25, 2027 for national supported-coverage engineering**. This is an explicit
planning assumption, not a statistically calibrated estimate or public launch date.

For a slower capacity scenario, 19 catalog states at three days plus the other
18 at five days gives 147 days, approximately March 2, 2027. That arithmetic is
not a deadline allocation: catalog states needing substantial implementation
still get the five-day activation window. Individual deadlines are assigned only
when activated. Finish early and update the forecast after five more acceptances
or a material blocker; do not spend unused budget deliberately.

Mobile stability, distribution credentials, privacy/support assets and store
approval remain additional public-release gates. They may proceed alongside
state work but cannot be assumed complete on the national engineering date.
October 20–27 remains a provisional beta estimate only. Ten-year history and
complete statewide claims do not block honest supported-coverage acceptance.
