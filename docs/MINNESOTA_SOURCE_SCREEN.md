# Minnesota source screen — September 14, 2026

The existing app has one geocoded [official winner release](https://www.mnlottery.com/news-release-record-breaking-408-972-north-nbsp-5-jackpot)
and a dated active Scratch Game catalog snapshot. The six-hour publisher now
imports the games listed on the official [Scratch Games page](https://www.mnlottery.com/games/scratch)
(40 at screening),
validating each detail page's game number, price and printed top prize. This
is a catalog of currently listed games, not claims, remaining prizes, or a
complete statewide winner count. The [Winners page](https://www.mnlottery.com/winners/game)
offers game, week, type, prize, and retailer filters, but its displayed
retailer names and cities alone do not verify every street address or a
complete record archive. The [Unclaimed Prizes page](https://www.mnlottery.com/games/unclaimed-prizes)
states that it lists lotto cash prizes of at least $25,000 and Scratch Game
prizes of at least $500; remaining/unclaimed prizes are not winning tickets
sold or claims made. Scratch Game detail pages publish printed prize counts
and warn that actual winners may differ with sales, distribution, and claims.

The [Lottery's data-requests page](https://www.mnlottery.com/about-the-lottery/data-requests)
directs written public-data requests to `datapractices@mnlottery.com` and
notes that winner street addresses and some winner names are private. Its
[public request policy](https://mnlottery-craft-assets-prod.s3.us-gov-west-1.amazonaws.com/DPPolicyReqFormMembers-of-the-Public_Update_4-10-19-REV6-3-19REV12-22-20rev4-29-26.pdf)
permits an emailed request without the form when it specifies the data, date
range, and inspection/copy preference. It permits electronic copies if kept
electronically and may charge for copies; a new compilation is not required.

On September 14, 2026, an emailed Minnesota Government Data Practices Act
request sought existing electronic draw and Scratch Game counts, game and
prize-tier definitions, public retailer IDs and addresses, and daily-capable
access for January 1, 2025–September 13, 2026 plus the current active Scratch
snapshot. It excludes private winner information and requests advance notice
of charges. Gmail confirmed “Message sent.” No complete all-tier ticket
count or live retailer join has been verified; Minnesota is not yet ready for
a complete statewide ranking or heat-map test.
