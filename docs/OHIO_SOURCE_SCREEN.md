# Ohio source screen — September 14, 2026

The Ohio Lottery's [Prizes Remaining report](https://www.ohiolottery.com/Games/ScratchOffs/Prizes-Remaining)
lists original and unclaimed Scratch-Off prizes and says the figures are current
as of approximately 6:00 a.m. daily. It also warns that tickets may appear
available when their claims have not yet been filed. This is a useful daily
inventory snapshot, but it is not a count of all tickets won or claimed during
a period.

The official [drawings page](https://www.ohiolottery.com/winning-numbers/drawings)
provides winning numbers and links to past numbers and payouts. Public news and
winner features cover selected larger prizes, so they cannot establish all-tier
winning-ticket totals. Retailer and cashing-location pages likewise do not
establish a complete machine-readable active retailer directory with stable IDs
and a join from each winning ticket.

publishes `public.records@lottery.ohio.gov` as the Records Administrator route.
It says email delivery is free and a database record exists when the system is
already programmed to produce it through ordinary sorting, filtering or query
features.
