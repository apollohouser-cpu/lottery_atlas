# Mississippi source screen — September 14, 2026

The [winning-number archive](https://www.mslottery.com/browse-winning-numbers/)
shows draw-date “Winners” detail controls, but the Lottery warns that the
numbers and prize information are unofficial until certified and that the
prize details apply to tickets purchased at a Mississippi retailer. No
complete multi-game, all-tier ticket export or certified correction behavior
has been established. Its [winner-release archive](https://www.mslottery.com/winninggame/scratch-off/)
names selected Scratch-Off wins and retailers, not all tickets or a complete
retailer ranking. The [retailer lookup](https://www.mslottery.com/retailers/find-a-retailer/)
says its directory was last updated September 1, 2026; it does not by itself
link every retailer to winning tickets.

The Lottery's [consolidated odds PDF](https://www.mslottery.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/MLC-2021-26-ConsolidatedOdds-02-26.pdf?x81279=)
has Scratch-Off game and prize-tier “# Of Winners” figures, but explicitly
says the number of prizes is established at printing and changes as prizes
are won. Top-prize counts may include second-chance promotional prizes.
Those are not verified sold winning tickets or claims and cannot be added to
actual draw counts as an up-to-date statewide total.

The [Public Records Request Policy](https://www.mslottery.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2023-02-21PublicRecordsRequestCLEAN.pdf?x79197=)
requires a written formal request mailed to P.O. Box 321433, Flowood, MS
39232, with requestor name, address and telephone, and lists
`prrequests@mslot.org` for process/documentation inquiries. On September 14,
2026, an email inquiry was sent there asking whether electronic submission
is accepted and how to obtain existing all-tier draw/Scratch counts and
public retailer joins on a daily-capable cadence. Gmail confirmed “Message
sent.” This is **not** a filed formal mailed records request. No response,
complete count, or retailer-linked winner archive has been verified.
