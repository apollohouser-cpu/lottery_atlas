# California source screen — September 13, 2026

California is not yet approved for a complete verified winning-ticket total
or retailer heat map. The [official Scratchers page](https://www.calottery.com/en/scratchers)
lists current games and top prizes claimed and remaining; individual game
pages include prize tiers and remaining counts. Remaining prizes are not
winning tickets claimed during a specified period. The official site also
publishes draw-game results and selected winner announcements, but this
screen did not establish a complete winning-ticket count by game and tier or
selling retailer for all games.

The Lottery's retailer finder is useful for locating nearby stores, but a
current statewide active retailer export with verified identifiers and
coordinates was not established here. Do not derive a statewide ranking
from selected large-win notices.

On September 13, 2026, a California Public Records Act request was sent to
`PRACoordinator@calottery.com`, the contact explicitly identified in the
[Lottery's privacy policy](https://www.calottery.com/en/privacy-policy).
It requests existing draw and Scratchers ticket/claim counts, selling
retailer links, a statewide active retailer directory, data definitions and
publication cadence. Gmail confirmed “Message sent.” No new counts are
verified merely by submitting the request.
