# Florida source screen — September 12, 2026

Update September 14, 2026: The Lottery's Open Government office acknowledged
receipt of the September 13 public-records request and said it is under review.
It will respond with any associated costs. The acknowledgment contains no
winning-ticket or retailer dataset, so the status below remains unchanged.

Florida is **not yet ready for a complete verified heat map**. Existing official
draw links and partial, explicitly labeled winner snapshots remain available.

- The [official retailer page](https://floridalottery.com/business/retailers)
  describes a network of more than 13,000 locations. The [Where to Play
  locator](https://floridalottery.com/where-to-play) helps find locations, but
  this screen did not establish a downloadable current statewide directory
  with exact addresses and verified coordinates.
- The [official Scratch-Off catalog](https://floridalottery.com/games/scratch-offs)
  and [top remaining prizes page](https://floridalottery.com/games/scratch-offs/top-remaining-prizes)
  are public sources for games and prizes. Their displayed content requires a
  source-specific importer and validation before claiming complete coverage.
- The [official media resources](https://floridalottery.com/news) provide
  retailer information for selected draw-game top prizes and jackpot winners.
  These game-specific reports and the [recent winners page](https://floridalottery.com/winning/recent-winners)
  do not establish a comprehensive retailer-level claim feed across all games
  from January 1, 2026 onward.
- Existing `data/florida_winner_activity.initial.json` and
  `data/florida_historical_activity.initial.json` explicitly describe partial
  verified snapshots. They must not be presented as complete current activity.

The relaxed freshness policy would permit a weekly or monthly official feed
with a clear state-specific cadence and source date. Florida's blocker here is
coverage and verified geolocation, not a stated publishing interval. Recheck
the Lottery's official directory/export and comprehensive winner data before
promoting Florida to the verified statewide map.
