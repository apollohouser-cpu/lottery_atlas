official game links remain available in Lottery Atlas.

  statewide retailer export with exact addresses and verified coordinates.

  explicitly shows only a few recent winners who agreed to photographs. It
  cannot serve as comprehensive retailer-level winner activity from January
  1, 2026 onward; some entries are digital games with no selling retailer.

The dated official Scratcher table can be disclosed at its actual source date;
slower updates alone do not block inclusion. Complete retailer and physical
