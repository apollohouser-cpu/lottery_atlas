• Step 1.

    Select Quick Pick to generate your numbers randomly, OR go to Step 2 to select your numbers.

  • Step 2.

    Select 5 numbers between 1 and 52 and one Star Ball number between 1 and 10.

  • Step 3.

    Repeat number selection for up to 5 plays. Each play costs an additional $1, or $2 if the All Star Bonus was selected.

  • Step 4.

    For an additional $1 per play the All Star Bonus will multiply non-jackpot winnings by 2, 3, 4 or 5 times!

  • Step 5.

    Select Multi Draw (optional) if you would like to play the same numbers for more than one drawing, you can choose 2 to 24 consecutive drawings.

Classic Lotto fun for just a buck! Lotto America is Montana Lottery’s newest game, with jackpots that start at $2 million and the All-Star Bonus that can multiply your winnings up to five times!

Cost per Play: $1.00

Drawing Days: Mondays, Wednesdays & Saturdays at 8:59 p.m. Mountain Time. You have until 8:00 p.m. on draw days to purchase your Lotto America tickets for that drawing.

Jackpot: Starts at a guaranteed $2 million and grows each time the jackpot is not won.

Multiple Draws: You may play the same set of numbers for up to 24 consecutive drawings.

All Star Bonus: For an additional $1 per play, All Star Bonus provides the opportunity to multiply any Lotto America prize you win (excluding the jackpot) up to five times.
