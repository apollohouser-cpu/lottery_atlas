Why don’t you limit Montana Millionaire to Montana residents only?

The Montana Lottery is available to everyone of legal age, whether resident or visitor. Limiting the sale of a product to some people while excluding others is discrimination. It would also be contrary to our mission and place an undue burden on our retailers.

Why don’t you offer Montana Millionaire or another raffle game more than once per year?

The Montana Lottery has a large selection of games available every day of the year. We believe Montana Millionaire is special and successful because it’s exclusive. While we may introduce other raffle games in the future, currently we have made this strategic business decision based on detailed market research and analysis.

Why aren’t the Montana Millionaire drawings shown live?

The Montana Lottery uses a certified random number generator (RNG) to select winning numbers for Montana Millionaire. This ensures fairness and eliminates the possibility of human error. While the RNG process itself is not visually exciting, we understand that some players may be interested in being a part of this day. If you are interested in being on the premises as these winning numbers are being drawn, please contact Montana Lottery at 406-444-5825.

Buying Your Ticket

  • Tickets are $20 per play.
  • The final deadline to buy Montana Millionaire tickets for 2026 is December 27, 2026, at midnight. As Montana Millionaire is a limited-ticket game, once tickets are gone, they are GONE!
  • Tickets can be purchased at any Montana Lottery sales agent. Check convenience stores, bars/taverns, grocery, casinos, etc. They can also be purchased at all our self-service terminals throughout the state.
  • Tickets are printed sequentially from 000001 to 720000. The first person who buys a Montana Millionaire ticket will be number 000001. The last ticket sold will be numbered 720000. There are no play slips to complete or numbers to choose.

Quarter Million Monday

  • Every ticket sold up to the point of the Quarter Million Monday Drawing is entered into the $250,000 drawing.
  • Winners of the Quarter Million Monday Drawing are still eligible to win a $1,000,000 grand prize.
  • The Quarter Million Monday Drawing for 2026 will be held on November 30, 2026.
  • Odds for the Quarter Million Monday Drawings will depend on how many tickets are sold before the drawing deadline.

Instant Wins

  • 9,700 Montanans will win instantly this year.
  • A complete listing of where instant win prizes were sold will be posted to the WINNING NUMBERS tab above after the game is sold out.
  • Here’s how it works: Buy a Montana Millionaire ticket. If you are an instant prize winner, a notification will print near the bottom of your Montana Millionaire ticket showing the prize you have won. A coupon to redeem your instant win prize will print immediately following your Montana Millionaire ticket. Redeem your coupon immediately to claim your instant win.
  • Hold on to your actual Montana Millionaire ticket! It’s still eligible to win all the other prizes in the game, including the Quarter Million Monday Drawing and the Grand Prize.
